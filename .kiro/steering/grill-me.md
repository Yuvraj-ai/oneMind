# Grill Me

The same relentless interview as `#grill-with-docs`, but **stateless**: it saves nothing locally and builds no `CONTEXT.md`.

## When to use

Use when you are **not working in a working directory** — sharpening a plan, a design, a piece of writing, anything with no repo under it.

If you are in a working directory, use `#grill-with-docs` instead: it runs the same interview and leaves a paper trail, so it is strictly the better one.

## How it works

Invoke the grilling discipline directly:
- Ask hard questions, force precision
- One round at a time (3-7 questions per round)
- Facts are the agent's job, decisions are yours
- Challenge vague answers, name contradictions
- Stop when the idea is sharp enough to act on

No files are created or modified.
